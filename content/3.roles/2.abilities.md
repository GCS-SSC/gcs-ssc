# Role Abilities

The **Abilities** tab is where you define exactly what a user with this Role is allowed to do within the system.

## Understanding Abilities

An Ability is a specific action tied to a specific subject. For example:

- `Create` + `Transfer Payment`
- `Read` + `Users`
- `Delete` + `Role`

By checking the boxes on this tab, you are granting those specific powers to anyone who holds this Role.

### Global vs Agency Abilities

- **Global Roles**: If a Global Role is granted the `Read Users` ability, they can see _every_ user in the system, regardless of Agency.
- **Agency Roles**: If an Agency Role is granted the `Read Users` ability, they can _only_ see users that belong to their specific Agency. The system automatically enforces this restriction behind the scenes.

## How to Modify Abilities

1. Navigate to the Role Detail page.
2. Select the **Abilities** tab.
3. You will see a list of all available system subjects. Expand a subject to see the specific `Create`, `Read`, `Update`, and `Delete` toggles.
4. Check the abilities you want to grant this role.
5. The changes are saved immediately upon toggling the switch.

> **Best Practice:** Always follow the Principle of Least Privilege. Only grant the exact abilities a user needs to perform their day-to-day work. Do not grant `Delete` abilities to standard operational roles.
