# Role General Settings

The **General** tab for a Role is where you configure the identity and definition of the permission set you are building for your users.

## What is a Role?

A Role is essentially a bundle of "Abilities" (permissions) that you can assign to a User. Instead of giving a user 50 individual permissions, you group those permissions into a single "Agency Manager" role and assign it to them.

### Defining the Role

When creating or editing a role, you provide:

- **Bilingual Name**: What the role is called (e.g., "Reviewer", "Administrator").
- **Description**: A short outline of what this role should be used for, ensuring other administrators know who should get it.
- **Scope**: Is this role meant to apply _Globaly_ across the entire system, or is it _Agency-Scoped_? **Agency Administrators can only create Agency-Scoped roles.**

## How to Edit a Role

1. Navigate to the **Roles** tab from the main sidebar.
2. Select the Role you wish to edit from the table.
3. On the **General** Tab, click the **Edit** button in the dashboard header.
4. Modify the descriptive fields. Note that you cannot change a role's Scope once it has been created and assigned.
5. Save your changes.
