# Cost Categories & Line Items

The **Cost Categories** tab is where you build the financial dictionary that your Transfer Payments will draw from. By defining these at the Agency level, you ensure all funding programs use the same consistent financial language.

## What are Cost Categories?

Cost Categories represent the broad buckets of funding that your agency authorizes. Examples might include:

- _Personnel / Salaries_
- _Equipment & Hardware_
- _Travel Expenditures_

### Line Items

Each Cost Category can have multiple **Line Items** nested within it. These are the specific, granular expenses that fall under the broader category.

- **Example**: Under the _Travel Expenditures_ category, you might have Line Items for _Airfare_, _Lodging_, and _Per Diems_.

## How to Manage Cost Categories

### Adding a New Category

1. Navigate to your Agency Detail page and select the **Cost Categories** tab.
2. Click the **New Cost Category** button.
3. Provide the bilingual name of the category.
4. Click **Save**. The category is now available to be linked to Transfer Payment Streams.

### Adding a Line Item to a Category

1. Locate the existing Cost Category in the table.
2. Click the specific category to open its nested **Line Items** view.
3. Click **New Line Item**.
4. Provide the bilingual name for the specific expense type.
5. Save the configuration.

> **Important Note:** Once a Cost Category or Line Item is actively being used by a live Transfer Payment, you cannot delete it. You may only deactivate or rename it to preserve historical financial integrity.
