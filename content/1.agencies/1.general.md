# Agency General Settings

The **General** tab under your Agency dashboard is where you configure the core identity of your organization within the GCS-SSC platform.

## What is an Agency Profile?

An agency profile contains the highest-level information that identifies your organization to the rest of the system. Ensuring this data is accurate is critical, as it populates headers, reports, and role assignments throughout the platform.

### Information You Can Manage

1. **Bilingual Name**: The name of your agency in both English and French. The system will automatically display the correct language based on the user's preference.
2. **Organization Identifier**: The formal abbreviation or acronym used to identify your agency (e.g., "SSC", "GCS").
3. **Status**: Whether your agency is currently `Active` or `Inactive`. Inactive agencies cannot be assigned new Transfer Payments or abilities.

## How to Update Your Profile

1. Navigate to **Agencies** from the main sidebar.
2. Click on your Agency's name in the table to open the Detail view.
3. Ensure you are on the **General** tab.
4. If your Role permits, you will see an **Edit** button in the top right. Click this to open the update modal.
5. Make your adjustments and click **Save**. The dashboard will immediately reflect your new agency details.
