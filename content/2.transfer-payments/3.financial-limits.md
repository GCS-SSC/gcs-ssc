# Financial Limits

The **Financial Limits** tab allows you to enforce strict guardrails on how much funding can be distributed within a specific Stream.

## Understanding Limits

Limits determine the acceptable bounds for grant applications and allocations within a stream. By defining these, the system automatically prevents users from approving funding cases that exceed their authorized caps.

### Types of Limits

- **Minimum Value**: The smallest amount of funding that can be requested or awarded per case.
- **Maximum Value**: The absolute ceiling for a single funding case.
- **Total Stream Value**: The collective maximum amount that can be distributed across _all_ active cases within this stream for a given fiscal year.

## Managing Limits

1. Ensure you are viewing the specific **Stream** you wish to configure.
2. Select the **Financial Limits** tab.
3. Click **New Limit**.
4. Input the numerical thresholds.
5. Save the configuration.

> **Warning:** Adjusting these limits after funding cases have already been approved may cause validation warnings on historical records. It is highly recommended to finalize limits before opening a stream to applications.
