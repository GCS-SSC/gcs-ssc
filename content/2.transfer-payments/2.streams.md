# Funding Streams

The **Streams** tab enables you to break down a large Transfer Payment Profile into specific, manageable funding channels.

## What is a Stream?

If the Transfer Payment Profile is the "National Research Grant", a **Stream** would be "Undergraduate Scholarships" or "Post-Doctoral Equipment Grants". Streams are where the actual specific rules, eligible recipients, and financial limits are applied.

### Configuration

When managing a Stream, you define:

- **Bilingual Name**: The specific name of this chunk of funding.
- **Reference Code**: A short identifier used internally for reporting.
- **Budget Link**: Connecting this stream to an internal Agency Cost Category.

## How to Add a Stream

1. Navigate to your Transfer Payment Profile.
2. Select the **Streams** tab.
3. Click **New Stream**.
4. Fill out the descriptive fields and link it to the relevant Agency Program.
5. Click **Save**.

Once a stream is created, you must proceed to the **Financial Limits** and **Eligible Recipients** tabs to define exactly how this stream distributes its funds.
