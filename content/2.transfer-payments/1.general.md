# Transfer Payment General Overview

The **General** tab for a Transfer Payment Profile acts as the main focal point for managing a specific funding program.

## What is a Transfer Payment Profile?

A Transfer Payment Profile represents the overarching program that your agency is funding (for example, "The National Research Grant Program"). The actual money is not managed here, but rather, this profile serves to group all related funding "Streams" under one logical umbrella.

### High-Level Fields

When you create or update a Transfer Payment via the General tab, you configure:

- **Bilingual Title**: The name of the funding program in English and French.
- **Active Status**: Determines whether this funding program is currently taking applications or actively managing funding allocations.

## Best Practices

- Keep titles broad enough to encompass all of the Streams that will eventually be nested underneath it.
- Use the **Active** toggle to pause an entire program. Deactivating the Profile cascades down, effectively pausing all associated Streams and Limit operations.
